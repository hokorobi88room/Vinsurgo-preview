You want to change, but where do you begin? 3 ways to describe your ideal self

Before searching for a method, find the one part of everyday life you genuinely want to change.

Written and edited by vinsurgo Editorial Team Published 22/8/2026 Updated 22/8/2026 Information checked 22/8/2026 6 min read

This article is for you if

You want to change yourself, but cannot decide whether to begin with work, learning, money or everyday life.

By the end, you will be able to

You can describe the day you want in your own words and choose one small change to test first.

If you are thinking, “I cannot stay like this, but I cannot see where to go”

Searching for courses, habits and success stories can make the destination less clear. You do not need a perfect life plan. You need one scene that would feel different in your own day.

The conclusion first.
  • Describe an ideal day, not an impressive title.
  • Choose the one area that casts the heaviest shadow over today.
  • Turn it into a change you can observe within seven days.

1. Picture an ideal day, not an ideal title

Ask what a better morning, working hour and evening would look like. If, six months from now, you opened your eyes with quiet anticipation rather than heaviness, what would be different? Write the room you see, the voices you hear and the work in your hands.

2. Choose the one place that hurts most

Trying to repair everything at once makes every method hard to continue. Perhaps you lack confidence at work, money feels tight, or no time belongs to you. Choose the one issue that most strongly shapes your present day.

An assessment can offer clues, but it is not a verdict. The useful question is not “Is this result correct?” but “Why did this result stay with me?” Your response helps you hear your own priorities.

3. Define a small change you can see in seven days

Test before making a large commitment. Watch thirty minutes of introductory material, read five job descriptions, or protect fifteen minutes before sleep. The action may be small, but you can observe how it changes you.

If, seven nights from now, you can breathe and say, “I can see the direction a little more clearly,” that feeling can support the next decision.

Connect the three methods to the life you want

Picture the day, choose the hardest point, and shrink the change until you can see it within a week. Together, these steps turn “I want to change” into something you can move today.

If next week’s morning feels slightly lighter, you set one boundary, or you recover fifteen minutes for yourself, your ideal has begun to enter real life. Begin with the feeling you want to regain, not with a grand title.

Move closer to the person you want to become

  • Describe one day you want six months from now: morning, work and evening.
  • Choose one priority from work, money, time or relationships.
  • Decide on one action whose effect you can observe within seven days.
