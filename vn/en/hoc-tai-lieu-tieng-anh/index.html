Study with English materials without losing the meaning

Preview the terminology, use subtitles purposefully and explain again in Vietnamese to check understanding.

Written and edited by vinsurgo editorial team Published 8/25/2026 Updated 8/25/2026 Information checked 8/25/2026 3 min read

This article is for you if

You understand the topic but get tired quickly or miss details when studying in English.

By the end, you will be able to

The process of understanding terms, checking subtitles and re-expressing them in Vietnamese.

Watching the entire video does not mean the meaning has remained

Speed of speech, new terminology, and the desire to increase the progress bar can cause you to catch words but lose relationships between ideas. Time passes, but the vague background appears as an error during practice.

Short conclusion

  • Preview the logical terms of the article.
  • Use subtitles to check, do not copy everything.
  • Re-explained in Vietnamese with new examples.
  • Check the language, subtitles and assessment format on the current course.

1. Create a term map before a long lecture

Read the module’s objectives and choose five to ten terms. Write short working meanings, noting when a word has a technical meaning different from its everyday meaning. You don’t need to translate the entire previous article; Just create a “hook” to follow the relationship between ideas.

In the first round, listen to understand the argument. On the second short turn, check the miss. Once the keyword is recognized, attention shifts from “what does this word mean?” to “why is this step necessary?”.

There’s no need to pressure yourself to sound like a native speaker. The goal is to understand it precisely enough to use it and recognize the limits.

2. Use subtitles and notes purposefully

Check the audio language, subtitles, transcripts and review language on the actual listing as each show is different and subject to change. Automatic subtitles or translation support, but technical jargon can still be wrong.

After a paragraph, stop and write a central sentence. Don’t copy the original; Taking too many notes can hide your lack of understanding of cause and effect relationships. Highlight any doubts and look up official documents or trusted professional sources.

A truly clear concept is better than four videos that just play as background sound. You are protecting quality, not learning unnecessarily slowly.

3. Demonstrate understanding by paraphrasing

Close the document and explain as if talking to a colleague who has not yet studied. Use Vietnamese first, adding examples from reports, inventory, services or plans near you. If just repeating English terms doesn’t create an example, go back to the unclear part.

Create a bi-lingual glossary of root words, meanings in context, examples, and common errors. For regulated industry words, check the official source. Record a two-minute explanation to hear where you’re still going around in circles.

When you can speak calmly in your own words, the translation has fulfilled its supporting role and the knowledge begins to belong to you.

Summary: supporting translation, supporting explanation

Previews reduce the burden, subtitles help with checking, and rephrasing detects gaps. Three steps to help you not give up because of the language but also not assume you understand just because the video ends.

To get closer to who you want to become

  • See objectives and terminology before each module.
  • Check the actual language and subtitle format.
  • Write a focus sentence after each section.
  • Look up suspicious terms in reliable sources.
  • Explain again in Vietnamese with your example.
  • Build a small glossary that can be used at work.
